## Scope Rule

Package proposals are reusable only after they are mapped to current app IDs, current source categories, existing matrix targets, review gates, and human-review boundaries. Example-specific, PEL-specific, trail/corridor-specific, incomplete, unsupported, or unsafe content is rejected or deferred here rather than copied into product behavior.
