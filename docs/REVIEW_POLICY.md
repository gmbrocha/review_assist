# Review Policy

Generated findings are not final until reviewed by a human professional.

The system may eventually draft findings, surface evidence, and prepare editable report materials. Human reviewers remain responsible for interpretation, correction, acceptance, rejection, and release.

Generated sections should not be blank simply because evidence is limited. The system may draft "no mapped conflict identified," "source unavailable," or "manual review required" findings, but those are still reviewable draft records.

## Future Finding Statuses

### draft

The finding has been generated or entered but has not yet been reviewed.

### needs_review

The finding requires human review before it can be used.

### accepted

A human reviewer has accepted the finding for use in the review package.

### rejected

A human reviewer has rejected the finding.

### edited

A human reviewer has modified the finding.

### unable_to_verify

A human reviewer could not verify the finding with available information.

## Policy Notes

- Findings should preserve source provenance.
- Findings should preserve uncertainty and missing-data flags.
- Imagery-observed features should be treated as review items, not authoritative facts.
- Restricted cultural resource status should be visible without exposing sensitive details in inappropriate outputs.
- LLM-generated narrative should be traceable to structured findings and remain editable.
- Draft report language should not imply final approval or final conclusions.
- Review status should be visible wherever findings are displayed or exported.
